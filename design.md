# ARMS PRO Design Notes

## Color Direction

The site should balance warm architectural neutrals with steel structural tones. The warm accent remains the primary highlight color; the steel colors support navbar/footer surfaces, overlays, borders, and subdued UI.

## Steel / Structural Palette

- Steel Charcoal `#20282B` - navbar, footer, strong dark sections.
- Deep Steel `#2D373B` - hero overlays and alternate dark surfaces.
- Steel Gray `#59666B` - supporting UI, captions, and quiet text moments.
- Muted Metal `#879297` - secondary metal tone for subdued details.
- Light Steel `#C8CED0` - borders and muted structural accents.
- Steel Mist `#E3E6E5` - light muted backgrounds.
- Warm Accent `#B89A68` - primary CTA, small labels, highlights, and active navigation.

Keep the palette restrained: warm architectural neutrals plus steel structural tones, not a fully blue-gray website.
